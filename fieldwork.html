Fieldwork

My research is grounded in fieldwork and collaboration with communities, researchers, and organizations in the places I study, across my work in development research, policy, and academia. These photographs document some of the people, places, and research processes that have shaped that work over the last 16 years. All photos were taken with consent, and most were taken by me. Where I appear in a photograph, it was taken by another person participating in or attending the activity.
